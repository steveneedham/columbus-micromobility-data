For Civic Leaders

This data helps the Short North Civic Association track micromobility activity and community complaints in your area. Use this information to:

  • Monitor parking and placement issues with bikes/scooters
  • Coordinate with operators (Veo, Spin) on compliance
  • Support data-driven advocacy with city planning
  • Identify problem areas for community engagement
